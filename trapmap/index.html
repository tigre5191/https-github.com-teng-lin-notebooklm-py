About

TrapMap is a personal, Waze-style trap map. Police reports are temporary and expire; Trap spots are places police regularly sit; Cameras are fixed speed / red-light cameras. “📡 Cameras” loads publicly-mapped speed cameras for the visible area from OpenStreetMap. Unlike Waze there is no global crowd behind this app — reports come from you and anyone you swap export files with. Long-press the map to add a spot anywhere. Alerts only fire while the app is open (keep it on the dash in Drive mode).

Your personal speed-trap radar:

  • 👮 Police — tap when you spot one; it marks your location and expires after a few hours
  • 🪤 Trap spot — save places police regularly hide
  • 📷 Cameras — load known speed cameras in your area from OpenStreetMap
